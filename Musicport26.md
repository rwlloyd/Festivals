## Dressing-room allocation notes

DR Times reserve each room from 45 minutes before to 45 minutes after the stage time. Room assignments do not overlap in the same room; allow at least 15 minutes to reset before the next booking.

- Rooms 1 and 2 hold 2-3 people each. A band may take both (written "1 + 2") for mixed parties of 4-6; Room 3 (up to about 6, with 2 private changing areas) is used when free; Room 4 is for parties over 6. Ibibio also gets Room 1 as overflow.
- Peace Artistes' size of 3 comes from your earlier draft; the parade party with the Eko team is unconfirmed, so Room 4 on Saturday morning is provisional.
- Gnawa Blues All Stars' rider gives no headcount; 5 is an estimate from its stage plot. If they exceed 6, Room 4 would clash with Ibibio's 2045 start, so the Sunday evening allocation would need revisiting.
- Saigon Soul Revival's 7-person travel party includes the tour manager; the band lineup lists 6 musicians.
- Hope and Social's rider says 8 players; your earlier draft said 4, so I've used the rider.

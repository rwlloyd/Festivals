## Dressing-room allocation notes

DR Times reserve each room from 45 minutes before to 45 minutes after the stage time. Room assignments do not overlap in the same room; allow at least 15 minutes to reset before the next booking.

- Rooms 1 and 2 are assigned to parties of 2-3 where possible; Room 3 is used for parties up to 6; Room 4 is reserved for parties larger than 6 and unconfirmed larger groups.
- Peace Artistes' rider does not state a headcount. Friday Room 3 is provisional and assumes 6 or fewer; if they exceed 6, the Friday booking conflicts with Hope and Social's need for Room 4.
- Gnawa Blues All Stars' rider gives no headcount; 5 is an estimate from its stage plot. Sunday Room 3 is provisional and assumes 6 or fewer; if they exceed 6, its call window abuts Ibibio Sound Machine's Room 4 booking and the allocation needs revision.
- Saigon Soul Revival's 7-person travel party includes the tour manager; the band lineup lists 6 musicians.

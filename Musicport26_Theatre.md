## Dressing-room allocation notes

DR Times reserve the room from 30 minutes before to 15 minutes after the stage time, shorter than the 45/45 used on the Main Stage. This is what keeps a single room workable: the shortest gap between Theatre sets is 70 minutes (Sunday, Marigolds to Danto Aya), which still leaves 25 minutes to reset between bookings. Gaps elsewhere are 75-90 minutes.

- Only DR1 is booked, so no two acts are ever in the dressing rooms at the same time.
- Headcounts are unknown for everything except Hannah Sanders & Ben Savage (3). Please confirm party sizes; if an act is too big for DR1, use DR2 for that act only, which will not clash with anything.
- The tightest handover is Sunday afternoon: Marigolds leave at 1435 and Danto Aya arrive at 1500 (25 minutes).
- Debasmita Bhattacharya is also at the Main Stage on Sunday morning (see [Musicport26.md](./Musicport26.md)), but her Theatre set is Saturday.
